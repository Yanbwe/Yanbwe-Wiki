# Command Reference

All commands use `/stylizeddamage` as the root.

---

## `/stylizeddamage reload`

Hot-reload all config files.

- **Permission**: 2 (admin only)

---

## `/stylizeddamage test <type> <amount>`

Spawn a test damage number at the crosshair.

- **Permission**: 1 (cheats only)
- **Example**: `/stylizeddamage test minecraft:in_fire 15`

---

## `/stylizeddamage list`

List all loaded styles and selector bindings.

- **Permission**: 1 (cheats only)

---

## `/stylizeddamage toggle`

Toggle damage number display on/off.

- **Permission**: 1 (cheats only)
